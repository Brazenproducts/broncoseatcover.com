Frequently Asked Questions

Do Wildtrak seats have any unique features that affect seat cover fitment?

The Wildtrak Edition is primarily a styling and equipment package — the front seats are the same shell as the standard Bronco. No special seat cover SKU is needed. The main fitment consideration is still 2-door vs 4-door rear bench, not the Wildtrak trim designation.

When did Ford release the Heritage Edition Bronco?

The Heritage Edition launched for the 2023 model year as a throwback to the original 1966 Bronco styling. It uses a retro two-tone paint scheme and specific interior details, but the seat architecture is the same as other Bronco trims. Standard Bronco seat covers fit without modification.
