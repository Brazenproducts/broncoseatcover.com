Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the 2-door Bronco rarer on the road than the 4-door?

Ford sold far more 4-door Broncos than 2-doors. The 4-door dominates retail sales due to family practicality. The 2-door appeals to serious off-roaders who value the shorter wheelbase for technical trail work. As a result, 2-door seat cover patterns are less common on Amazon — fewer brands bother with the separate rear bench pattern.

Can I use 4-door rear covers on a 2-door Bronco?

No. The 4-door rear bench is wider and longer. 4-door rear covers will bunch and fit poorly on the smaller 2-door bench. Front covers are interchangeable — rear covers are not. Always specify 2-door when ordering rear seat covers.
